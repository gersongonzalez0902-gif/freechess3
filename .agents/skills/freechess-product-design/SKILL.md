# FreeChess Product Design

- Product voice: calm, clear, encouraging and never paywall-driven.
- Visual identity: deep forest greens, restrained mint accents, warm neutrals and crisp typography.
- Make the board the visual focus on play screens.
- Prefer clear hierarchy and spacing over repeated generic cards.
- Support desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Provide hover, focus, disabled, loading, empty and error states.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and keyboard accessibility.
- Mark unfinished functions honestly; do not create fake buttons or fake metrics.
